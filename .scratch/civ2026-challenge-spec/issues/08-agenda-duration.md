# Is the agenda three hours or three and a half?

Type: grilling
Status: open

## Question

`HACKATHON-HANDOFF.md` §8: 3-hour fixed agenda, or 3.5 hours with a longer pitch and quality
review?

§4.2 currently allocates the three hours as 30/30/45/30/30/15, and §4.2 notes that extra time
should go to investigation and pitch review — never to installation.

What has to be settled:

- Is the slot length actually ours to choose, or is it fixed by the CIV 2026 programme? (This
  may be a fact rather than a decision — check the programme before grilling.)
- If 3.5 hours: does the extra 30 minutes go to the Challenge stage (failure investigation) or
  to Transfer (pitch and review)?
- How many teams present, and how long does turnover between pitches actually take? Six teams
  at three minutes plus turnover does not fit in the current 15-minute Transfer block.
- Does the BYOD decision in ticket 04 demand a buffer at the front for setup failures — and if
  so, is that buffer inside the slot or a pre-event session?

## Why this is on the frontier

Nothing blocks it, and it constrains the run-of-show, the coach guide and the checkpoint
timings currently sitting in the map's fog.
