# Viewer layouts

Open these layouts with the matching recording in Lichtblick or PlotJuggler.
Follow the [participant setup guide](../docs/setup.md) for viewer setup and the
[kit viewer guide](../kit/viewer/README.md) for the supplied assets.

| Recording | Lichtblick | PlotJuggler |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kashiwa right turn | [JSON layout](lichtblick-kashiwa-right-turn.json) | [XML layout](kashiwa-right-turn.pj4.xml) |
| Reims left turn | [JSON layout](lichtblick-reims-left-turn.json) | [XML layout](reims-left-turn.pj4.xml) |

The layouts configure views of recorded signals. They do not define detector conditions or scoring rules.
