## Post-event feedback and rework, September–October 2026

After the hackathon, feedback called for a clearer first run, simpler website and guides, less fragile setup, and an explicit distinction between practice labels and private organizer evaluation. The [post-session map](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/56) framed this continuation, sometimes called “Phase 6” or “next-wave” in working notes. Its documentation, code, dataset and release tracks overlapped; the names do not describe one newly accepted event phase. A [focused code audit](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/59) also led to bounded parsing, path-safety and validator fixes, including [merged runtime cleanup](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/pull/94).

For participants, the rework established short audience entry points and current Markdown guides as the source for kit copies and website summaries. It simplified the participant page, added section navigation, clarified extension and scoring references, and documented connected-first installation with offline recovery. The [documentation decision](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/60), [merged participant consistency work](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/pull/150) and [website acceptance](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/105) record the path from feedback to reviewed material; organizer and presentation copies were then checked in a [local kit candidate](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/106).

For organizers, the work added a source-bound label acquisition and review workflow and defined complete practice coverage for designated sources while keeping sealed evaluation truth private. The [practice-label decision](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/64), [organizer workflow](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/65) and [both-dataset qualification](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/110) record that progression. Later guidance recommends organizer-chosen timing and access for source-bound practice labels; it does not distribute a label payload ([#127](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/127)).

The [implementation map](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/109) and its platform epics tracked separate checks through mid-October. Accepted installation and recovery evidence covers native Windows AMD64 and local WSL2 Linux AMD64; bare-host Linux and externally enforced network denial remain unverified ([#112](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/112)). The refreshed local kit passed its recorded inventory, link and workflow checks, and [clearance preparation](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/119) and [final acceptance](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/68) were closed for their stated scope. These results do not themselves publish the repository, distribute the kit or private inputs, deploy the site, or extend platform support.

### Post Rework TLDR

#### Added

- Introduced local event-candidate review with interval editing and separate practice-label and private-truth exports. Human review and missed-event checks make the resulting labels a reviewed reference for each designated recording. [Organizer label review, PR #97](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/pull/97).
- Created a reusable label-acquisition skill and synthetic helper. Organizers can follow the same preparation and review steps for another session, with explicit handling of practice labels and private truth. [Acquisition workflow, #65](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/65).
- Enabled persistent section navigation and guarded left/right arrow keys on the participant website. Participants can move between instructions during the workshop while controls retain their own keyboard behavior. [Participant navigation, PR #149](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/pull/149).

#### Changed

- Simplified the reading paths for participants, organizers, and contributors. Current Markdown guides own the instructions, kit copies carry those guides, and the website provides shorter summaries, reducing conflicting directions across materials. [Documentation structure, #60](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/60).
- Streamlined setup around connected-first installation, direct participant commands, and matching offline recovery packages. Recorded checks cover native Windows AMD64 and local WSL2 Linux AMD64, giving organizers specific recovery routes they can prepare for those environments. Bare-host Linux and externally enforced network denial remain unverified. [Platform qualification, #112](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/112).
- Aligned participant copy, organizer guidance, and presentation material with the approved workflow, then verified their inclusion in a local kit. Readers can use the guides and decks together without relying only on source-level consistency. [Material and kit integration, #106](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/106).
- Clarified complete practice-label coverage for each designated source and organizer control over sharing time. Participants can compare results against matching reviewed labels, while organizers retain control of access and keep evaluation truth private. [Practice-label sharing, #127](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/127).
- Extended kit preparation with practical third-party notices and checks of inventory, checksums, and local links in the refreshed candidate. Those checks establish which materials were assembled and reviewed without implying permission to distribute the kit or its inputs. [Kit clearance preparation, #119](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/119).
- Unified participant command parsing and scoring-reference parsing through shared implementations. Both CLI entry points can use the same behavior, while organizer scoring retains its additional provenance checks. [Shared runtime behavior, PR #94](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/pull/94).
- Consolidated mapping and string validation across organizer tools. Reusing the existing validators reduces the places maintainers must update without changing the recorded diagnostics or artifact outputs. [Organizer validator reuse, PR #96](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/pull/96).

#### Removed

- Retired invitations to submit through the historical form or USB hand-in route. The workflow now ends with a validated local submission ZIP and hash, so a reader does not mistake the old event's collection route for an available service. [Retired intake guidance, PR #155](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/pull/155).

#### Fixed

- Rejected non-finite durations and invalid evidence windows before artifact generation. Participants receive an input error before an invalid configuration produces output files. [Duration and evidence-window validation, #72](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/72).
- Preserved the last valid live-page snapshot when polling fails. A failed refresh no longer requires discarding the last successfully received session information. [Live-page recovery, #73](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/73).

#### Security

- Blocked unsafe archive paths before submission extraction, including Windows drive and stream paths. Checking raw archive names prevents Windows normalization from hiding a rejected path and avoids partially extracting an invalid submission. [Windows intake safety, PR #95](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/pull/95).

Read [current participant contracts](../reference/participant-contracts.md) and [scoring rules](../reference/scoring.md) for the active reference. The original v1-only wording and ROS-required acceptance commands remain historical evidence. They do not override current tooling or the runtime qualified for another session. New v2 output preserves the recorded compatibility boundary; intake does not execute submitted trace or test content.

## Record scope and release boundaries

This is documentation navigation under the [confirmed public-content boundary](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/67), not final clearance of the repository or its history. The selected records describe tooling, aggregate acceptance evidence and recorded limitations. Dataset-specific source reports and records with unresolved private-path or source-derived disclosure findings are outside this index. Their existing files remain untouched for the separate clearance process.

The original event's human approvals apply only to the exact inputs, audience and use recorded there. They do not grant another session data rights, expanded platform support, legal clearance, or a new public release. Recording terms, restricted imagery, sealed truth, reviewer audit and operational access material retain their own boundaries. Publication of an existing path is not authorization to distribute its inputs.

[Public-payload/history clearance](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/119) and the [final release gate](https://github.com/Forerunner2012/civ2026-data-hackathon/issues/68) remain separate. Replacing a current file does not remove its earlier Git objects. This index changes no repository visibility, hosting, dataset availability or history.
